Notation

How the notation works
| ana - reviews | paper
two nodes, a labelled link from ana to paper
| ana - knows - | sam
- | makes the link undirected
| a | b | c
a chain of links
| paper @date 2026-11-03
a note on a node (on a link: before its |)
@kind person
its icon: person, place, thing, action, event, idea
@src world
a card for the model's extras: world, guess, advice
| old = new
rename (or merge); | old = deletes
# heading
any line not starting with | is a comment
